1. Operational Diagnostic: Identifying Current Bottlenecks

As an ANSI-approved standards body managing Standard 01023 (Training Providers) and Standard 01024 (Instructors), IREC currently suffers from extreme process fragmentation. Staff spend hundreds of hours manually shuttling data between Asana, Salesforce, Google Drive, Excel JTA crosswalks, and an outdated IREC Direct portal.

📊 Accreditation Cycle Time Breakdown (Days)

Comparing process durations in legacy manual architecture versus the proposed automated Canvas ecosystem.

Key Takeaway: Eliminating manual document reviews and missing notification loops compresses a 216-day cycle into under 24 days.
01

Inquiry & Eligibility Blindspot

Candidates express interest via untracked emails logged into Asana. Fillable Word documents open in Google Drive frequently break formatting, corrupting data and forcing weeks of back-and-forth emails.

02

Application & Financial Fragment

100+ documents are uploaded into IREC Direct in an unorganized list. Disconnected PayPal payments and 90% DOE Weatherization subsidies require manual Salesforce overrides and Google Sheet trackers.

03

Desk Assessment Communication Silence

Assessors and candidates communicate in a shared Word document without automated email notifications. Both parties must manually check the portal continuously, risking candidacy expiration.

04

Post-Award Disconnect

Staff manually design PDF certificates in Canva. Lack of API sync requires re-typing public listings from Asana forms into Green Workforce Connect website registries and Google Sheets.

2. Licensing Models: MAU Bundle vs. Academic Enrollment

Because IREC's training volume is heavily dependent on cyclical grant funding and public subsidies, selecting the correct software licensing model is critical for long-term fiscal health.

Canvas Career Bundle

Monthly Active User (MAU) Model

The Analogy: Flexible Gym Membership

You do not pay for every person who ever registered at the front desk over the past decade. You only pay for individuals who scan their badge and "work out" in any given month.

  • ✔ Bundles Full Suite: Includes Canvas LMS, Ignite AI, Studio, & Catalog
  • ✔ 10,000 Historical Records: Stored at zero licensing cost when inactive

Canvas Academic

Provisioned Enrollment Model

The Analogy: Annual Apartment Lease

You pay a fixed, flat rate for a specific physical seat for the entire year, regardless of how often the student is actually in the room or taking courses.

  • ✖ Core LMS Only: Catalog & Studio require separate paid extensions
  • ✖ Reactivation Penalty: Activating historical users consumes annual seats
  • ✖ 1,000 Enrollment Tier: Strictly capped at 1,000 total enrollments/yr
